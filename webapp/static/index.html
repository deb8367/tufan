Trans-Boundary Stubble Smoke Migration Model (Punjab/Haryana → Delhi NCR)

Step 1 Agricultural Crop Residue Fires

Post-paddy harvest burning in Amritsar, Firozpur, Sangrur, and Kaithal generates high particulate plumes rich in carbonaceous aerosols.

Step 2 North-Westerly Wind Advection

Seasonal upper-air winds travel at 15–25 km/h from the north-west, carrying uninhibited particulate streams across the Indo-Gangetic Plain.

Step 3 Thermal Inversion & Basin Trapping

Declining night temperatures lower the atmospheric boundary layer to ~480m, preventing vertical dispersal and trapping smoke in Delhi's urban bowl.
